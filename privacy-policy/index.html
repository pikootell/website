Privacy Policy

Last updated July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pikootell ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our applications published on Google Play, including our Wear OS watch faces and our Android games (collectively, the "Apps"). By using our Apps, you agree to the practices described below.

Summary

  • Watch faces: we do not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal data.
  • Games: may display third-party ads and offer in-app purchases, which involve limited data processing by our partners (described below).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information collected

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or contacts through the Apps. However, some of our games rely on third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device and usage information (e.g. device model, operating system version, language, and app interactions).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) used to serve and measure ads.
  • Diagnostics and crash data used to keep the Apps stable.

Advertising

Our games may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (for example, Google AdMob and its partners). These networks may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure their performance. We do not control the data collected by these networks; their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies, including Google's advertising policies.

You can limit ad personalization at any time by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

In-app purchases

Some Apps offer optional in-app purchases and subscriptions. All payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase information is handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy.

How information is used and shared

Information processed by our third-party partners is used to operate and improve the Apps, display and measure advertising, process purchases, and maintain security and stability. We share information only with these service providers as needed to provide the Apps, or where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/EEA/UK) or CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal information and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we do not maintain our own user accounts or databases, most requests relating to advertising and purchases should be directed to the relevant provider (Google). You may also contact us using the details below and we will assist where we are able.

Data retention

We do not retain personal data ourselves. Any data processed by our third-party partners is retained in accordance with their respective policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at pikootell@gmail.com.
