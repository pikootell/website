About Clearway: Unblock Puzzle

Clearway is a clean, relaxing logic puzzle about untangling a board full of snakes. Every snake faces one direction. Tap it to send it sliding off the grid, but only if the path ahead is completely clear. Solve the tangle by finding the right order to move them, one snake at a time, until the board is empty.

It starts gentle and grows with you. Early boards are small and forgiving, so anyone can pick them up in seconds. Later levels fill wider grids with longer, overlapping snakes that block each other in every direction, turning each board into a satisfying knot to unravel. No timers pressuring you, no reflexes required, just you and the puzzle.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Each snake points up, down, left, or right.
  • Tap a snake to slide it off the edge in the direction it faces.
  • A snake can only leave if no other snake is blocking its path.
  • Clear the whole board to solve the level.
  • Think a few moves ahead: freeing one snake opens the way for the next.

FEATURES

  • Hundreds of handcrafted-feeling levels with a smooth difficulty curve.
  • Simple one-tap controls that anyone can learn instantly.
  • A calm, minimalist 3D look with smooth, tactile animations.
  • Earn up to three stars per level by solving without hints or mistakes.
  • Level up and track your progress as you master tougher boards.
  • Optional hints when a board has you stuck.
  • Unlockable snake skins in a range of colors and styles to make the board your own.
  • Play offline, anywhere, no internet connection needed.
  • Your progress is saved automatically on your device.

MADE FOR SHORT SESSIONS AND LONG ONES

Clear a quick level while you wait in line, or settle in and push through a harder stretch. Clearway fits whatever time you have. It is a great fit for fans of sliding-block puzzles, unblock and untangle games, brain teasers, and minimalist logic games who want something thoughtful without the clutter.

A LIGHT PROGRESSION SYSTEM

Play through levels using hearts that refill over time, so it is easy to take a break and come back. Hints can help you spot the next move on a tricky board. An optional one-time Premium unlock removes ads, unlocks every skin, gives you free hints, and adds extra hearts that refill faster, but it is completely optional, and every level can be solved with the free version.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT

  • No pressure: no clocks, no losing streaks, no punishment for thinking.
  • Truly pick-up-and-play: the rules are clear from the very first board.
  • Puzzles that reward planning over luck. Every level has a solution.
  • A tidy, distraction-free design that stays out of the way of the puzzle.

Clearway contains ads and offers an optional in-app purchase. Both are entirely optional and the full set of levels is playable for free.
